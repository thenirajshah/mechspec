This site organises flange dimension data according to ASME B16.5 for quick engineering and procurement reference.

ASME B16.5

Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings (NPS ½ through NPS 24) is the primary standard referenced on MECHSPEC. It covers pressure–temperature ratings, materials, dimensions, tolerances, marking, and testing for pipe flanges and flanged fittings.

Class 150 Class 300 NPS ½″ – 24″ Dimensions in mm

What this reference covers

  • Outside diameter of flange (O)
  • Thickness of flange (Q)
  • Diameter of raised face (R)
  • Bore diameters for slip-on / socket weld and lap joint
  • Hub diameter and length through hub for welding neck, slip-on and lap joint
  • Bolt circle diameter, number of bolt holes and hole size

Pressure classes on this site

Class 150

Common low-to-moderate pressure service. 21 nominal sizes from ½″ to 24″ are included in the Dimension Finder.

Class 300

Higher pressure rating than Class 150. Matching size range is available with corresponding dimension sets.

Scope note

MECHSPEC is a practical lookup aid. It does not replace the controlled ASME/ANSI standard, manufacturer drawings, or project specifications. Always verify critical design, fabrication and procurement values against the governing documents before use.
